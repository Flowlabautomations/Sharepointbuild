🗓 First Day▼
Meet your manager and team, get your equipment set up, complete your system access forms, and attend the welcome briefing.
📅 First Week▼
Attend your departmental orientation, review key policies, complete mandatory online training modules, and schedule 1-on-1s with your team.
📆 First Month▼
Complete your 30-day performance check-in, finalise your role KPIs with your manager, and attend your first all-hands or town hall meeting.
🎯 First Quarter▼
Review your 90-day plan with your manager, meet your cross-functional teams, establish recurring check-ins, and collect feedback on your progress.
